Frequently Asked Questions

Everything we get asked most at the showroom. Can't find your answer here? Call or WhatsApp us directly and we'll help.

How do I know my size before visiting?

Most customers try things on in person at our Mirchaiya showroom, which is the most reliable way to get the right fit. If you can't visit right away, call or WhatsApp us your usual measurements and our team can help you estimate a size ahead of time.

Can I reserve an item over the phone?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us the product name (or send a link from this site) and we'll hold it for you for a short period so you have time to visit.

What is your exchange policy?

Items can be exchanged within a few days of purchase with the original tag and proof of purchase, subject to availability. Please ask our team in-store for the current exchange window when you buy.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash in-store, along with common Nepali digital payment options. Ask our team which methods are currently supported when you visit or call.

Do you offer home delivery?

Items are available for pickup at our Mirchaiya showroom. Call or WhatsApp us to ask about delivery options if you're elsewhere in the Siraha area.

Where are you located?

We're at Sanastha Chowk, Mirchaiya, Siraha, Madhesh Pradesh, Nepal. See the map and full directions on our Contact page.

What are your showroom hours?

We're open Sunday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and closed on Saturday. If you're planning a special trip, it's worth calling ahead.
